# main

`une main` ‘a hand’ is a feminine [simple noun](../../../simple-nouns.md).

Examples:

> Xavier soupira, [passa](../../../../verbs/p/pa/pas/passer.md) sur son crâne une <mark>main</mark> [fluette](../../../../adjectives/f/fl/flu/fluet.md). ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p1))
> > *‘Xavier ran a slender hand over his bald head’*

----

Latin **manus**, **manūs** *fem.* ‘a hand’.
