# crâne

`une crâne` ‘a skull, a cranium’ is a masculine [simple noun](../../../simple-nouns.md).

Examples:

> Xavier soupira, [passa](../../../../verbs/p/pa/pas/passer.md) sur son <mark>crâne</mark> une main fluette. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p1))
> > *‘Xavier ran a slender hand over his bald head’*

----

Medieval Latin **crānium**, **crāniī** *neut.* ‘a skull’; borrowed from Greek **κρᾱνῐ́ον**, **κρᾱνῐ́ου** *neut.* ‘a cranium, the upper part of the head’; diminutive of **κᾰ́ρᾱ**, **κᾰ́ρᾱτος** *neut.* ‘a head’.
