# lampe

`une lampe` ‘a lamp, a light, a bulb’ is a feminine [simple noun](../../../simple-nouns.md).

Examples:

> Seule, la <mark>lampe</mark> avait [changé](../../../../verbs/c/ch/cha/changer.md) : Mme Frontenac avait [acquis](../../../../verbs/a/ac/acq/acquérir.md) un [modèle](../../../m/mo/mod/modèle.md) nouveau que toute la famille [admirait](../../../../verbs/a/ad/adm/admirer.md). ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
> > *‘only the lamp had changed : she had acquired a new model which everyone admired’*

----

Latin **lampas** *fem.* **lampadis** ‘lamp, lantern, torch’; borrowed from Greek **λᾰμπᾰ́ς**, **λᾰμπᾰ́δος** *fem.* ‘a torch’; from **λᾰ́μπω** *vb.* ‘shine, be bright, give light’.
