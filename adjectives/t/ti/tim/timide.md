# timide

`timide` ‘shy, timid’ is a [simple adjective](../../../simple-adjectives.md).

Attributive examples:

> Xavier Frontenac jeta un [regard](../../../../nouns/r/re/reg/regard.md) <mark>timide</mark> sur sa belle-sœur qui tricotait. (*[François Mauriac](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p1)*)
> > *‘Xavier cast a timid glance at his sister-in-law’*

----

Latin **timidus**, **timida** *adj.* ‘fearful, timid, cowardly’; derived from **timeō**, **timēre** *vb.* ‘fear, be afraid’.
