# Le Mystère Frontenac – I

<a id="p1"></a>
> XAVIER FRONTENAC [jeta](../../../verbs/j/je/jet/jeter.md) un [regard](../../../nouns/r/re/reg/regard.md) timide sur sa belle-sœur qui [tricotait](../../../verbs/t/tr/tri/tricoter.md), le buste droit, sans s’[appuyer](../../../verbs/a/ap/app/appuyer.md) au dossier de la chaise basse qu’elle avait [rapprochée](../../../verbs/r/ra/rap/rapprocher.md) du feu ; et il [comprit](../../../verbs/c/co/com/comprendre.md) qu’elle était irritée. Il [chercha](../../../verbs/c/ch/che/chercher.md) à se [rappeler](../../../verbs/r/ra/rap/rappeler.md) ce qu’il avait [dit](../../../verbs/d/di/dir/dire.md), pendant le dîner : et ses propos lui semblèrent dénués de toute malice. Xavier [soupira](../../../verbs/s/so/sou/soupirer.md), passa sur son crâne une [main](../../../nouns/m/ma/mai/main.md) [fluette](../../../adjectives/f/fl/flu/fluet.md).


Xavier Frontenac cast a timid glance at his sister-in-law, who was knitting, sitting bolt upright without leaning against the back of the low chair she had drawn close to the fire; and he realised that she was annoyed. He tried to remember what he had said during dinner, but his remarks seemed to him entirely innocent. Xavier sighed and ran a slender hand over his bald head.

<a id="p2"></a>
> Ses yeux fixèrent le grand lit à colonnes torses où, huit ans plus tôt, son frère aîné, Michel Frontenac, avait souffert cette interminable agonie.
