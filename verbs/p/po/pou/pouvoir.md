# pouvoir

`pouvoir` ‘can, be able to’ is a (modal auxiliary) [simple verb](../../../simple-verbs.md). 

Negative abilitative examples:

> Il revit les mouches inlassables de juin qu’il ne <mark>pouvait</mark> chasser de cette face suante. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
> > He had been unable to brush away the tireless June flies from that sweating face saw again the head thrown back, the enormous neck engulfed by the thick growth of a vigorous young beard; the tireless June flies that he had been unable to brush away from that sweating face.
>
> Xavier ne <mark>pouvait</mark> plus détourner les yeux de ce lit ni de ces murs. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
