# pouvoir

`pouvoir` ‘can, be able to’ is a (modal auxiliary) [simple verb](../../../simple-verbs.md). 

Negative abilitative examples:

> Il revit les mouches inlassables de juin qu’il ne <mark>pouvait</mark> [chasser](../../../c/ch/cha/chasser.md) de cette face suante. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
> > *‘He had been (physically) unable to brush away the flies from the face’*
>
> Xavier ne <mark>pouvait</mark> plus [détourner](../../../d/de/det/détourner.md) les yeux de ce lit ni de ces murs. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
> > *‘Xavier was no longer (physically) able to (or still unable to) tear his eyes away from the bed or the walls’*

----

Latin **possum**, **posse** *vb.* ‘be able to, can’.
