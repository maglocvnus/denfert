# passer

`passer` ‘pass, go through/past’ is a [simple verb](../../../simple-verbs.md).

Perfective examples:

> Xavier soupira, <mark>passa</mark> sur son [crâne](../../../../nouns/c/cr/cra/crâne.md) une [main](../../../../nouns/m/ma/mai/main.md) fluette. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p1))
> > *‘Xavier ran a slender hand over his bald head’*

----

Vulgar Latin **passō**, **passāre** *vb.* ‘go past/through’; derivative of **passus**, **passūs** *masc.* ‘a step, a pace’; participle of **pandō**, **pandere** *vb.* ‘spread out’.
