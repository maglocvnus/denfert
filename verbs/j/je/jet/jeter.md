# jeter

`jeter` ‘throw, cast’ is a [simple verb](../../../simple-verbs.md).

Perfective examples:

> Xavier Frontenac <mark>jeta</mark> un [regard](../../../../nouns/r/re/reg/regard.md) timide sur sa belle-sœur qui tricotait. <br/>
> *‘Xavier cast a timid glance at his sister-in-law’ – [François Mauriac](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p1)*

----

Etymology: Latin **iactō**, **iactāre** *vb.* ‘scatter’; frequentive derivative of **iaciō**, **iacere** *vb.* ‘throw’.
