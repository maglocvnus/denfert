# détourner

`détourner` ‘...’ is a [simple verb](../../../simple-verbs.md). 

Examples:

> Xavier ne <mark>pouvait</mark> plus [détourner](../../../d/de/det/détourner.md) les yeux de ce lit ni de ces murs. ([*François Mauriac*](../../../../texts/francois-mauriac/le-mystere-frontenac/ch1.md#p2))
> > *‘Xavier was no longer (physically) able to (or still unable to) tear his eyes away from the bed or the walls’*

Latin **captus**, **capta** *adj.* ‘captured, seized’.
